## 🕊️ Why Swaasa Midha Dhyasa Exists

In a world filled with constant noise, screens, and sensory overload, **Swaasa Midha Dhyasa** (Telugu/Sanskrit: *"Mindfulness and Focus on the Breath"*) was created to offer a peaceful haven for your mind.

It is designed to help you:
- 🌿 **Release Anxiety & Tension** in moments of overwhelming stress.
- 🎯 **Restore Deep Concentration & Focus** before demanding tasks.
- 🌙 **Prepare Your Body for Restful Sleep** after long days.
- 🧘 **Cultivate Daily Mindfulness** through sacred audio-visual harmony.

## 🫁 What It Is Made Of

### 🔬 1. Science-Backed Pulmonary Rhythms
Swaasa includes proven breathing methods developed by neurobiologists and wellness experts:
- **Box Breathing** (`4 · 4 · 4 · 4`): Equalized breathing used to clear mind fatigue and reset focus.
- **4–7–8 Sleep Breath** (`4 · 7 · 8`): Extends exhalation to naturally slow your heart rate for sleep.
- **Physiological Sigh** (`2 · 1 · 4` double-inhale): Stanford neurobiology method that rapidly offloads stress within seconds.
- **HRV Coherence Breath** (`5.5 · 5.5`): Harmonizes your heart rate rhythm with your natural breath.

---

### 📳 2. Tactile Haptic Breath Guidance
Feel your breath without looking at the screen. On mobile devices, gentle tactile vibration pulses expand smoothly as you inhale, tick gently during holds, and release softly as you exhale — allowing you to practice comfortably with your eyes closed.

---

### 🎵 3. Rich Soundscapes & Music Layering
- **YouTube Music Streaming**: Play your favorite ambient tracks or custom YouTube Music URLs directly in the app.
- **Procedural Acoustic Soundscapes**: Layer organic rain, ocean waves, or authentic Indian **Tanpura Shruti Box drones** and **Carnatic Raga Swaras** (Veena & Bamboo Flute) underneath your music with a custom balance slider.
- **Organic Airflow Audio**: Soft, natural human breath prompts replace harsh digital beeps.

---

### 📱 4. Install Anywhere, Works Offline
Swaasa is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA). You can install it directly onto your iPhone, Android phone, tablet, or laptop — so your personal mindfulness sanctuary is always available, even without an internet connection.
